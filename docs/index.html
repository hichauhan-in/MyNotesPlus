MyNotes+ Privacy Policy

Private. Encrypted. Yours.

Last updated: 7 September 2026

MyNotes+, developed by Himanshu Chauhan, is an offline-first notes, reminders, drawing and personal expense-tracking app. This policy explains its local storage, optional Google services, sharing, and data controls.

Information we collect

No MyNotes account required

You can create and edit notes without signing in. The app handles the content you enter, including text, images, recordings, handwriting, reminders, account names, balances and transaction records. The developer does not operate a server that receives your note content. Optional Google services and their SDKs have the data practices described below.

  • Your content and preferences are stored in app-private storage. Optional sync and sharing can transmit content you choose.
  • Connecting Drive stores your Google account email and sync configuration locally. OAuth access tokens are used in memory to authorize Drive requests.
  • Expense balances and transfers are manual records. The app does not connect to banks, initiate bank payments, trade investments, or provide financial advice.

How your notes are stored

Note titles and bodies, saved attachment files, template names and bodies, and reminder titles and bodies are encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Local encryption keys are held in the Android Keystore, hardware-backed where supported. Content is decrypted in memory for display, search, reminders, widgets, sync, or a sharing action.

Organization metadata, including tags, book names, record IDs, note types, timestamps and settings, is not all encrypted by the app, but remains in app-private storage. Camera captures and recordings use temporary private files before encryption. Decrypted attachments may be cached in memory. Readable exports and ordinary share files are intentionally created when you request them.

Up to 20 recent saved versions of each note retain encrypted title/body content and attachment references for recovery. Undo history is held in memory during editing. Receipt recognition runs on-device; suggested amounts and dates are reviewed before saving, and no bank payment is made. Content shared from another app is imported only after you choose Save or Append inside MyNotes+.

On-device assistance uses Google ML Kit for OCR, smart suggestions and handwriting recognition, and Gemini Nano through AICore on supported devices for summaries. Recognition and inference process your content on-device; the app does not send that content to a cloud AI API. Some models download from Google. ML Kit can send diagnostic and usage data to Google, including device and app information, identifiers, performance metrics, feature events, error codes, configured languages and input/output sizes. This can occur even when Drive sync is off. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's Privacy Policy.

Exports are readable files in Downloads/MyNotes or your chosen folder. Ordinary sharing can create readable files in the app's private share cache and grant the receiving app temporary read access. Encrypted sharing instead creates a passphrase-protected file. The receiving app's own privacy and retention practices apply to anything you share.

Because the key stays bound to your device and never leaves it, the encrypted note database and its attachments are excluded from Android’s automatic cloud backup and device‑to‑device transfer. This keeps your notes on the device that created them; it also means that installing the app on a new device (or using Android's system restore) starts with an empty library rather than notes that could no longer be unlocked. Updating the app on the same device keeps all your existing notes and unlocks them normally.

Encrypted local backups are separate from Android system backup. When requested, MyNotes+ creates a passphrase-protected .mynotesbackup containing notes, books, media, templates, reminders and recent versions. Records use AES-256-GCM; a PBKDF2-derived key protects the archive. The temporary archive is encrypted, and the file is written only to your chosen document destination, which may itself be cloud-backed. Restore stages media encrypted with this device's key and adds fresh copies only after validation and confirmation. Existing security settings and Drive credentials are not restored. Anyone with the backup and its passphrase can read its contents. Limits include 32 MB per archive entry and 512 MB of total payload.

Permissions we use

Biometric (USE_BIOMETRIC) Used only if you turn on App lock, to unlock the app with your fingerprint, face, or device screen lock. Biometric data is handled entirely by the Android system — the app never sees or stores it.
Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) Requested when you choose to record a voice note. Recordings are stored privately and encrypted after recording. They can leave the device if you explicitly export or share them, including inside an encrypted share package.
Network access (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) Used for optional Google Drive authorization, encrypted sync, and confirmed public-link sharing, and by Google SDKs for model downloads and diagnostics. Turning sync off does not disable all SDK network traffic.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) Used to show reminders you create. If permission is denied, reminders remain in the app but notifications cannot be delivered. Notification content is private on the lock screen, subject to system settings. With app lock enabled, notification and widget text is replaced with a generic reminder label.
Alarms & reboot (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) Used to schedule reminders and re-arm them after restart. Exact-alarm access is optional; without it, Android may delay delivery. Reminder text is encrypted locally and included in optional encrypted Drive sync.

The app requests no location or contacts permissions, and no camera permission of its own - photos are added through the Android system photo picker or camera app.

Optional end‑to‑end encrypted Google Drive sync

Off by default

Cloud sync is disabled by default and only ever runs if you deliberately turn it on and connect a Google account. When enabled, every note (of any type), your saved templates and your reminders are encrypted on your device before they are uploaded, so only unreadable ciphertext blobs are stored in your Google Drive.

The key that unlocks your notes is protected by a recovery passphrase that you choose and is kept only in wrapped (encrypted) form. Because the key is never uploaded in the clear, neither Google nor anyone you might accidentally share a Drive folder with can read your notes — only someone with your account and passphrase can. Signing in on a new device with the same account and passphrase restores the supported synced records.

Current sync limits: note content, custom templates and reminders sync. Image and audio attachment binaries, recent note versions and book/folder structure do not yet sync. Use a full encrypted backup when moving all your data. Conflicting note edits may produce an independent conflict copy; this is not collaborative editing or a merged financial ledger. Drive also receives account authorization details and file metadata such as identifiers and modification times; these are not hidden by content encryption.

Google Drive’s own privacy policy also applies to the encrypted blobs stored in your account. If you lose your recovery passphrase, the synced notes cannot be recovered — this is the trade‑off that keeps them private to you.

“Share a link” is separate and always a deliberate choice. When you tap Share → Share a link on a note, that single note is uploaded to your own Google Drive as a readable copy and given an “anyone with the link can view” permission so you can hand the link to someone. Unlike synced notes, this copy is not end‑to‑end encrypted — anyone who has the link can open it. Nothing is shared until you confirm the public-link warning. Expense trackers cannot be shared as public links. The public copy is a snapshot, not live collaboration. Delete it from the "MyNotes Shared" folder in Drive to revoke the link; this cannot recall copies already downloaded.

“Share encrypted” is the private alternative. Choosing Share → Share encrypted packs the note into a passphrase‑locked .mynote file that is encrypted on your device before it leaves the app. MyNotes does not upload it automatically; your chosen receiving app may upload or retain it. New shares require at least 12 passphrase characters. Send the passphrase through a separate channel. Import supports packages up to 16 MB, individual attachments up to 6 MB, and 8 MB of attachment data in total. Imported attachments receive new names so they cannot overwrite files belonging to another note.

Data retention & deletion

Notes, books and templates can be moved to Trash, restored, or permanently deleted. The default trash-retention period is 30 days and can be changed in the app; cleanup runs when the app runs. Reminders can be deleted from the Reminders screen. Local data otherwise remains until you remove it.

Permanent note deletion also removes its saved versions. Media still referenced by another note, template or version is retained. Unreferenced files are eligible for cleanup after a 48-hour grace period while the app runs. Cancelling a prepared restore removes its staged media; interrupted preparation may leave encrypted files for later cleanup. Backup files in your chosen destination are separate copies and must be deleted there, including any provider-held copies.

  1. Remove local app data: open Settings > Security & Privacy > Data controls, then Device storage. Use Android's Storage > Clear storage, or uninstall MyNotes+. Back up anything you need first. Exported files in Downloads or a chosen folder are separate and must be deleted there.
  2. Remove Drive data: disconnect Drive on every device first. In Drive, delete the MyNotes backup folder and any MyNotes Shared copies, then empty Drive Trash. In Drive Settings > Manage apps, find MyNotes and delete its hidden app data to remove the recovery-key envelope, templates and reminders. Google controls its own retention policies.
  3. Revoke access: remove MyNotes from your Google account's third-party connections. Disconnecting or revoking access does not itself delete existing Drive files or local notes.

There is no separately hosted MyNotes user account to close. Connecting Drive uses your existing Google account; MyNotes does not delete that account. Readable share-cache files are temporary and can also be removed through Android's Clear cache control. Recipients control their own copies.

Security

We use industry‑standard protections including AES‑256‑GCM encryption, the Android Keystore, and an optional biometric/device-credential app lock. Authentication errors do not open the vault. You can also enable "Hide in recent apps" to protect previews. No storage or security mechanism can guarantee absolute protection. Keep your device secure and your recovery passphrase private.

Children’s privacy

MyNotes+ is a general-purpose productivity app, not specifically designed for children. Its Google SDK data practices are described above and are not exempted by a user's age. Contact the developer with concerns about children's personal information. Store target-audience declarations must accurately reflect the audiences for which the app is distributed.
